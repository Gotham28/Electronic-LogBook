# Arogya Progress Coach

The Progress Coach is an AI assistant designed to help residents review their training progress against department requirements.

It analyzes your verified and pending logs to provide personalized tips on what to focus on next.

## Facts Used
The Progress Coach uses the following facts to generate tips:
- Your verified and pending case logs grouped by category
- Your verified and pending procedure logs grouped by procedure type
- Your verified and pending academic activities grouped by type
- Your department's targets and requirements for procedures and academic activities

The coach does not have access to clinical details, patient text, or your personal leave records.
